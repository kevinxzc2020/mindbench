# Individual test and game covers

Generated on 2026-11-10 with the built-in ImageGen tool. 33 distinct final images: 8 cognitive tests, 14 device checks/history entries, 1 record-and-replay tool, and 10 mini-games. IQ, MBTI and tarot use their existing dedicated light laboratory images.

These are conceptual cover illustrations, not screenshots or diagnostic results. Each final JPEG is an uncropped, full-resolution export of its individual generated PNG. The source PNGs remain in the Codex generated_images directory. No montage was split into multiple covers.

## Prompt set

Full shared prompt, per-entry subjects, source filenames and the targeted click-speed correction are recorded in [manifest.json](./manifest.json).
